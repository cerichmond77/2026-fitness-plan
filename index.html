Six-Month Progressive Plan · Jun 1 – Nov 28, 2026

From a twenty-minute jog
to real, lasting fitness.

A focused but sickness-aware build. Three days a week becomes four. Twenty minutes becomes sixty. You hit forty-five minutes by month three, then spend three months adding intensity — HIIT, supersets, sharper intervals. Cardio, dumbbell strength, and yoga rotate every week, with a deload every fourth week so your body keeps building instead of breaking down. Click any day to see the workout.

Workout types

Cardio — brisk walk, intervals, light jog
Strength — dumbbells + bodyweight
Yoga / Mobility — recovery, flexibility, breath
HIIT — short, high-intensity intervals
Deload week — every 4th week, ~70% effort

The yellow-flag rule

If you wake up feeling run down, scratchy throat, unusually sore, or poorly slept — swap the day for an easy yoga / mobility session, or just take the dog for a longer walk.

Never make it up. The deload weeks already account for a missed session here and there. Pushing through illness is exactly what sets you back six weeks.
